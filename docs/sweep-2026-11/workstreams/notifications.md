### <a id="part-44"></a>PART-44 — Due-task notifications fire once per todo, not once per kind or per due date
*medium · `needs-decision` · effort S.* **Where:** `src/utils/notifications.ts:536`; `src/utils/notifications.ts:575`; `src/hooks/useTaskNotifications.ts:115`. **Problem.** There are three kinds of notification (due soon, due today, overdue), but the record of what was notified is keyed by todo id alone. The owner confirms that the extra notifications are wanted. **Done when.** Unit test: a todo gets 'due soon' and then 'overdue'; after rescheduling to tomorrow, it gets 'due soon' again.
