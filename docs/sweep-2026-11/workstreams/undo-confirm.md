### <a id="dup-04"></a>DUP-04 — Soft-delete, archive and undo lifecycle copied three times; undoing a delete duplicates the id
*high · `bugfix` · effort M.* **Where:** `src/hooks/useTodos.ts:193-216`; `src/hooks/useTodos.ts:604-672`; `src/hooks/useNotes.ts:153-187`; `src/hooks/useNotes.ts:325-370`; `src/hooks/useReviews.ts:46-78`; `src/hooks/useReviews.ts:229-273`; `src/hooks/useUndoableActions.ts:200-216`; `src/hooks/useTodos.ts:584-586`. **Problem.** handleFinalize, the undo-delete branch, delete, archive and the settingsModel memo are near-identical across useTodos, useNotes and useReviews. Only the noun differs. All three undo-delete branches prepend the restored copy while the soft-deleted entry stays in the array, because useUndoableActions.undo never finalizes. Two entries then share an id, every later prev.map rewrites both, and the duplicate is persisted: toggling after undo shows the task twice. archiveTodo also re-inlines notifyDependencyBlock. **Proposal.** Add src/utils/lifecycle.ts with markDeleted, markArchived and restoreWithActivity. Add useSoftDeleteUndo&lt;T>({ setItems, label, makeActivity }), returning finalize/undo handlers plus deleteItem and archiveItem. Undo-delete does `[restored, ...prev.filter(x => x.id !== id)]`. useTodos layers complete/uncomplete undo, the recurring-spawn removal and canArchive on top. Unarchive targets stay per entity: todos go to completed or active, notes to active, reviews to completed. **Done when.** A reproducer per hook (delete, undo, toggle or edit) asserts exactly one entity with that id in state and in storage.

### <a id="bug-04"></a>BUG-04 — Undoing a delete leaves the soft-deleted copy, creating duplicate-id todos, notes and reviews
*high · `bugfix` · effort S.* **Where:** `src/hooks/useTodos.ts:204-216`; `src/hooks/useNotes.ts:162-171`; `src/hooks/useReviews.ts:55-64`; `src/hooks/useUndoableActions.ts:200-217`; `src/hooks/useTodos.ts:584-586`. **Problem.** Delete keeps the record with state 'deleted'. Undo clears the finalize timer and prepends a restored copy, so two records share one id. Any later toggleTodo, archiveTodo, archiveNote or archiveReview replaces both, making the ghost a visible, persisted duplicate with a duplicate React key. **Proposal.** In each handleUndo 'delete' branch, replace the record by id (prev.map), and fall back to prepending only when the id is absent. **Done when.** For todos, notes and reviews: deleteX, undo, then toggle or archive leaves exactly one record with that id.

### <a id="log-04"></a>LOG-04 — [Cross-dimension: logic] Undoing a delete leaves two records with the same id, and later edits turn them into two visible copies
*high · `bugfix` · effort S.* **Where:** `src/hooks/useTodos.ts:610-627`; `src/hooks/useTodos.ts:204-216`; `src/hooks/useUndoableActions.ts:213-229`; `src/hooks/useNotes.ts:166-170`; `src/hooks/useNotes.ts:341`; `src/hooks/useReviews.ts:59-63`; `src/hooks/useReviews.ts:246`. **Problem.** deleteTodo keeps the record in the list with state 'deleted' until finalize runs. Undo calls only onUndo, and handleUndo prepends the restored copy, so two records share one id. The next toggle, archive or workflow move maps by id and replaces both, leaving two identical visible tasks. Those are persisted and survive a reload. Notes and reviews follow the same pattern, and React gets duplicate keys.
